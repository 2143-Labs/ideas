**Weapons**
*Net Launcher* - 1 shot per reload, shoots a net projectile that roots the first target it hits preventing movement abilities, but can catch multiple enemies together if they are stacked.

*Magic 8 Ball* - Infinite ammo bowling ball that you roll that goes in a straight line and bounces a few times off of stuff it hits.  

*Icy Snowball* - Allows you to chuck frozen snowballs with ice chunks inside that slow enemies on hit and can sometimes crit (headshot).

**Armor / Equipment**

*Thermal Goggles* - Throws a filter on that changes screen to be thermal vision with enemy highlighting in white. (Like the T-7s from Tarkov). Can be attached to certain helmets.

*Ohtani Jersey* (+Strength, +Luck) - Should go over a existing chest armor and be a baseball jersey that gives bonus stats.

*Smelly Cleats* - Shoes that give extra movement speed and maybe a sprint ability that increases movement speed by 100% for a few seconds.



**Throwables**

*Singularity Core* - Throwable nade that activates a black hole that sucks in all spells and projectiles within x radius of the explosion for 10 seconds. Slows movement speed in radius.

*Semtex* - A sticky grenade that can stick to enemies, makes the beeping noise from MW2 and blinks red when thrown.

*Weighted Net* - Similar to the net launcher but its shorter range and a 1 time use, but can pick it back up to replenish it.
